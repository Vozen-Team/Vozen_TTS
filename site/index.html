    Features

    Everything your server needs

    One bot. Every voice feature you wished Discord had — and the core is free forever

    Auto-read channel

    Pick a channel and Vozen reads every message out loud. Set it up once with /setup

    Your voice, your language

    Every member picks their own voice from 35 languages — Google or a local neural engine

    Speaks like a human

    Uses your chosen voice by default. Optional per-message detection is enabled with /voice detection on; ! and CAPS add emphasis

    16 voice minigames

    Guess the language, spelling bees, Wordle, reflex duels — with a server leaderboard

    Personality included

    Greets people who join — without repeating itself — wishes happy birthday, tells jokes, answers the magic 8-ball, and tracks your server's top speakers

    Admin peace of mind

    Blocklists, rate limits, an anti-spam filter that mutes flooded messages, role gating, opt-out and a kill-switch. You stay in control

    NEW

    Voice effects

    Robot, echo, deep, chipmunk, radio, demon… your messages, transformed. Two effects free, all eight with Premium

    Fair Premium

    €6.99/month unlocks Kokoro and the server-wide extras. The essential Piper-powered core stays free

    NEW

    Private tools, anywhere

    Use /tts-file and /translate text, or right-click a message to translate or transcribe supported Discord audio — privately, without joining a call

    Channel profiles

    Give each channel its own voice, engine, language, speed, limits and voice-channel binding from the secure server dashboard

    Favourite voices

    Save up to 25 favourites and jump back to your 10 most recently used voices without searching the full library again

    Open source

    All of Vozen's code is public on GitHub. Audit it, learn from it, or open a PR — no secrets, no spyware
